Election rules

One vote, once

Each exam number can vote a single time per election. There is no way to change a vote after it's submitted.

Your ballot is secret

Your exam number is checked to confirm eligibility, but it is never stored alongside your selections.

Results stay hidden until closing

No running counts or leading candidates are shown while voting is open — results publish only after the election closes.
